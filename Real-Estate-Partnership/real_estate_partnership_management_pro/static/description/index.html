Real Estate Partnership Management

Comprehensive real estate partnership management system for Odoo 15 that handles partner investments, property management, expense tracking, and profit distribution.

Key Features

  • Partner management with balance tracking
  • Property management with detailed specifications
  • Investment tracking per property
  • Expense management and distribution
  • Transaction management (deposits/withdrawals)
  • Property sale and profit distribution
  • Professional UI with organized views
  • Complete audit trail

Business Benefits

  • Streamlined partnership operations
  • Transparent financial tracking
  • Automated profit distribution
  • Comprehensive reporting capabilities
  • Multi-currency support
  • Role-based access control
  • Mobile-friendly interface
  • Scalable architecture

Module Capabilities

This module provides a complete solution for managing real estate partnerships without dependencies on standard Odoo modules. It includes:

  • Partner Management: Complete partner profiles with balance tracking, transaction history, and investment portfolio.
  • Property Management: Detailed property records with specifications, location, boundaries, and comprehensive documentation.
  • Investment Tracking: Flexible investment system with percentage calculations and withdrawal capabilities.
  • Expense Management: Categorized expenses with automatic partner distribution and payment tracking.
  • Financial Operations: Complete transaction management with deposits, withdrawals, and profit distributions.
  • Sale Management: Property sale process with automated profit calculation and distribution to partners.
